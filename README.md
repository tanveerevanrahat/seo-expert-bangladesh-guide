# How to Choose the Right SEO Expert in Bangladesh (2027)

Choosing an SEO expert is a high-stakes decision. The wrong one costs you months and money; the right one compounds growth for years. This guide gives you a practical framework — no jargon, no hype.

## The 8-point checklist

1. **Proof, not promises.** Ask for real results: traffic growth, keywords ranked, and case studies with numbers. "96K+ monthly organic traffic delivered" is proof. "We will rank you #1 in 30 days" is a red flag.
2. **Process.** A good expert explains their process: audit, strategy, technical fixes, content, links, reporting. If they cannot explain it, they probably do not have one.
3. **Realistic timelines.** SEO takes months. Anyone guaranteeing instant rankings is selling something else.
4. **Reporting.** You should see what was done, what changed, and what is next — in plain language, every month.
5. **Technical depth.** Content alone does not rank. The expert must handle site speed, indexing, schema, and site architecture.
6. **AI search knowledge.** In 2027, visibility means Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Gemini — not just classic rankings. Ask how they optimize for AI answers.
7. **Niche experience.** Results in your industry matter more than generic claims.
8. **Communication.** You should understand what you are paying for. Confusion is a warning sign.

## Red flags

- Guaranteed #1 rankings
- Secret methods they cannot explain
- Extremely cheap packages with no clear deliverables
- No reporting, or reports full of vanity metrics
- Fake reviews or fabricated case studies

## What good SEO looks like in practice

Good SEO is boring in the best way: consistent technical fixes, genuinely useful content that answers real questions, honest reporting, and steady growth you can verify in Search Console. For example, our work for RFL Eshop took monthly visitors from 389 to 96,600+ — documented, not claimed.
